How it works

From a long address to a short link in three steps

  1. 1

    Paste your long link

    Copy the address of any web page and paste it into the box above.

    example.com/blog/2026/how-we-planned-our-trip?utm_source=…
  2. 2

    Get a short link

    LinkLab creates a unique short name (or uses yours) and saves the pair.

    /trip-plan
  3. 3

    Share it, we redirect

    When someone opens the short link, LinkLab looks it up and forwards them to the original page.

    VisitorLinkLabOriginal page
